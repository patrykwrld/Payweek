Payweek prices every shift you work — night rates, weekend rates, breaks, shifts that run past midnight — so you know what you're owed before the money lands.

One email when it launches. Nothing else, ever.

Built for agency work, not office work

Weekly pay, several agencies, and rates that change depending on when you clocked in.

It knows your real rate

Set each agency up once. Night rate between any two times, weekend rate, a one-off rate when you've done a deal.

Midnight doesn't break it

A shift running 18:00 to 02:00 gets split across the right rates, and unpaid breaks come off the band they actually fall in.

Works in the car park

No signal, no problem — shifts save on your phone and sync when you reconnect. Big numbers you can read one-handed.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, nothing sold to anyone. Export everything to a spreadsheet or delete it, whenever you like.

Where it's up to

Payweek is finished and in closed testing on Google Play right now. Google requires a two-week test before a new app can go public, so it's a matter of weeks rather than months. Leave your email above and you'll get one message the day it's live.
