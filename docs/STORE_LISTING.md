## Full description

```
Payweek tells you one thing fast: what your pay week is looking like.

Built for agency workers paid weekly, across as many agencies as you juggle.
Log a shift in seconds, see what you have earned so far, and check your
payslip against what you were actually owed.

WORKS OUT YOUR REAL RATE
Set up each agency once and Payweek prices every minute you work:
• A night rate between any two times you choose
• A weekend rate for Saturdays and Sundays
• A one-off rate for a shift when you have done a special deal
• Shifts that run past midnight, split across the right rates
• Unpaid breaks taken out of the band they actually fall in

Tap any shift to see exactly how the figure was reached — every band, every
rate, every subtotal. No black box.

CATCH AN UNDERPAYMENT
Enter the gross from your payslip and Payweek tells you whether it matches or
how far short it is, with the shift-by-shift comparison behind the number.

BUILT FOR THE CAR PARK
• Add a shift in seconds, or repeat your last one with one tap
• Works with no signal — shifts save on your phone and sync when you reconnect
• Big, legible numbers you can read one-handed
• Holiday accrual shown as its own line, so you know what is building up
• Export everything to a spreadsheet whenever you want

NO NONSENSE
No ads. No tracking. Your data is yours — export it or delete it whenever you
like.

Payweek estimates gross pay from the hours and rates you enter. It does not
calculate tax or National Insurance, and it is not a substitute for your
payslip or advice from your agency.
```
